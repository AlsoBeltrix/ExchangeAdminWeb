### Implementer's approach (written before code, at `9762fef0`)

The sites, at `9762fef0` (`ExchangeAdminWeb.Tests/ExtendedLogServiceTests.cs`), each a wait for the
consumer - another thread - to do something it will do, bounded at 10 s of wall time:

| Line | Test | Waits for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 335 | an entry lost while its file is held | `SpinUntil(DroppedEntryCount == 1, 10 s)` |
| 433 | an entry dropped for want of the lock (`LockWait` 300 ms) | `SpinUntil(DroppedEntryCount == 1, 10 s)` |
| 532 | the rotation test, held entries written | `SpinUntil(Messages.Count == fillers + held, 10 s)` |
| 576, 580 | the folder-deleted test | `SpinUntil(Messages.Count == 1, 10 s)`; `SpinUntil(TryDelete(folder), 10 s)` |
| 647 | `AFullQueue_CountsTheEntriesItDiscards` | `entered.Wait(10 s)` - the consumer takes the first entry |
| 747, 762 | `Dispose_CountsWhatItCouldNotWriteInItsBound_BeforeItReturns` | `entered.Wait(10 s)`; `SpinUntil(Entries.Count == 1, 10 s)` |

Each is `Assert.True(<wait>, ...)` on a condition the code reaches by itself, so a longer bound only
makes a genuine failure slower to report; none waits for something NOT to happen. kd-16 gave the
same kind of wait in its own test (708) a literal 2 minutes.

Chosen, the kd-16 shape: one constant, `ConsumerWait` = 2 minutes - the value kd-16 chose, twelve
times the longest stall seen - used at all eight sites and at 708, so every wait this file makes on
the consumer is one knob. Test-only; `Services/ExtendedLogService.cs` untouched (aud-a64 (5)).

Left alone, and why (candidates, not this finding's list):
- 640, 695, 738 `release.Wait(60 s)`: the other direction - the consumer waiting for the TEST, a
  hold that must outlast the test's own steps; 60 s is six times the stall seen. In
  `AFullQueue_CountsTheEntriesItDiscards` and `Dispose_CountsWhat...` a hold that ran out early would
  change what is dropped, so a stall over 60 s would fail them.
- 654-658, the drain poll in `AFullQueue_CountsTheEntriesItDiscards`: 600 x 100 ms = 60 s for 1,025
  entries with no sleep in the writer (about 11 s at aud-a46's 10.98 ms append) - five times its
  normal cost, the most exposed bound in the file under heavy load.
- 801, `JsonlFileAppender.Lock(path, 10 s, ...)` in `HoldTodaysLock`: the test taking an
  uncontended lock in its own setup - not a wait on the consumer.

Files (declared): `ExchangeAdminWeb.Tests/ExtendedLogServiceTests.cs`, this record.

Proof, the kd-16 injected-delay method on 647: the consumer's first write delayed 12 s before it
signals `entered` - with the fix the test passes; with `ConsumerWait` back at 10 s it fails at "the
consumer never took the first entry"; restored byte-exact, it passes.
