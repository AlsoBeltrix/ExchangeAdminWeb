# kd-57: a cancelled lookup is filed Failed with the framework's own cancellation words

**Severity**: LOW, and partly an owner question. After kd-37 a cancelled Recipient Lookup is shown and audited through
the page's existing "lookup failed" path: result Failed, error "The operation was canceled." Message Trace words its own
("Cancelled by the operator"). Whether every cancelled read should say that, and whether a cancel should be filed Failed
at all rather than its own outcome, is a ruling nobody has taken.
**Status**: Fixed on `fix/kd44-a70-13` - settled the simple way, consistent with aud-a70 (13), by the supervisor's brief under the owner's 2026-10-27 mandate: a cancel is filed with the result "Cancelled" and the words "Cancelled by the operator" at every site a cancel is filed. Verified on branch; awaiting merge; unreviewed until the single final codex-commercial review
**Branch**: `fix/kd44-a70-13` (from master `3f7eaa07`, after kd-44's reversal and aud-a70 (13))
**Commit**: the commit that carries "As implemented" below (`git log -1 -- .agents/review/findings/kd-57.md`)
**Reviewer**: implementer (aud-a68/kd-2 agent, candidate; not yet confirmed by the supervisor); fixed by the kd-44/aud-a70 (13)/kd-57 agent

## Evidence

At `9ffc653c`; raised by the kd-37 agent from its own change.

## Approach (to be written by the implementing agent before code)

- Settle the wording and the result first (one shared phrase, and whether a cancel is Failed or a third outcome beside
  Success and Failed), then apply it wherever a cancel can be filed. If the result classification changes, it touches
  usage telemetry and the trace operation the same way aud-a70 (13) does - flag that to the owner rather than deciding it.

The owner question this left is answered by the 2026-10-27 mandate (`.agents/decisions.md`, item 0: the supervisor does not
bring the owner questions a plain reading of it answers) and the supervisor's brief: one phrase, Message Trace's; one result
string, "Cancelled"; no new outcome class in the models; nothing the operator can do changes. The usage and trace effect is
aud-a70 (13)'s, the commit before this one: only "Success" is a success, so a Cancelled row counts as an action that did not
succeed and completes its implicit trace operation as not succeeded, naming its result.

### As implemented (implementer, 2026-10-27; lines at `3f7eaa07`)

**The settlement**, in `Services/AuditService.cs`: `CancelledResult` ("Cancelled"), `CancelledByTheOperator` ("Cancelled by
the operator") and `FiledCancelled(extra)` - a copy of the caller's `extra` with `result` set to Cancelled. Through `extra`
because the methods a cancel is filed by take no explicit result, and `MergeExtra` is merged last: kd-6's
`FiledUndetermined` on the Blocked Senders page is the same move. The result is a string, as Ineligible is; `AuditResult`,
`PermissionResult` and `RecipientInfoResult` are unchanged.

**The sites.** Found by reading every site kd-37 and kd-61 touched - the Recipient Lookup page (229), `RecipientLookupService`,
`ExchangeServiceBase`'s on-premises location and size reads, `ModuleCredentialService` (kd-61's and kd-69's throws) - and by
a grep for `OperationCanceledException` and `IsCancellationRequested` handling through `Components` and `Services`. Three
places file a cancel:
1. **Recipient Lookup** (`Components/Pages/RecipientLookup.razor` `RunLookup`). The lookup's own catch took every throw, a
   cancel included, as a lookup that failed: `ResolveFailure(ex).primary` - "The operation was canceled." - shown, the
   activity completed with it, the row filed Failed. A catch ahead of it, `when (activity.CancellationToken.IsCancellationRequested)`,
   takes the operator's cancel: the error shown is "Cancelled by the operator", the activity completes with it (not
   succeeded), and the row is filed Cancelled with it as its error. A cancellation the operator did not ask for - the
   activity's token not cancelled - still goes down the failed path in its own words.
2. **Message Trace** (`Components/Pages/MessageTrace.razor` `DownloadSelectedDetails`). Its `catch (OperationCanceledException)`
   already said "Cancelled by the operator" (now the constant) and filed the row Failed; it files it Cancelled.
3. **Blocked Senders** (`Services/BlockedSenderService.cs` `UnblockSenderAsync`, `Components/Pages/BlockedSenders.razor`
   `ConfirmUnblock`). An unblock cancelled before it was sent came back as a failed result - "The unblock of X was
   cancelled before it was sent, so nothing was changed and the sender is still blocked." - filed Failed. The page could
   not tell it from a failure without a new outcome on the result, so the service throws it as what it is, an
   `OperationCanceledException`, worded "Cancelled by the operator before the unblock of X was sent, so nothing was changed
   and the sender is still blocked." The page catches it when its activity's token is cancelled, shows that sentence,
   completes its trace scope as not succeeded with it, files the row Cancelled (the servicer note kept, kd-10) and sends
   the administrator notification as before. A wait that ran out before the send stays a failed result, and a cancel
   after the send stays Undetermined: its outcome is unknown (kd-6, the aud-a73 rule), which a cancel does not change.

Read and not changed, because none files a row for a cancel: `ModuleCredentialService`, `DelineaService`,
`RecipientLookupService` and `ExchangeServiceBase`'s reads (they let a cancellation out to the page), Migration's reads
through `IsCancellation` (a cancelled read leaves the read and files nothing of its own), `DefenderEndpointDeviceService`,
`DefenderApiClient`, `OperatorMembershipDirectory`, `AdSiteDirectory`, `AdSiteSubnetMap`, `PowerShellDirectoryCommands`,
`GraphTokenClient` (each rethrows or stops). `BulkJobService` gives a cancelled job the status Cancelled and the message
"Cancelled by operator." - the job store's record, not an audit row. Admin Bulk Jobs' and Conference Rooms' cancel and
remove rows audit the cancel request itself (whether it was accepted), not a cancelled operation.

**What the operator can do is unchanged**: the cancel is the same navigation-guard OK, nothing is offered or withdrawn, and
nothing more or less is written. What changes is the words they are shown on Recipient Lookup and Blocked Senders, and the
result the three rows are filed under.

**Pages.** `RecipientLookup.razor` 254 -> 263 lines, `MessageTrace.razor` 1,370 -> 1,371, `BlockedSenders.razor` 567 -> 580.
All three are "not started" in `ClickGateRegistry` (no line-keyed entries), so nothing is re-anchored; their

**Versions.** Recipient Lookup 1.2.0 -> 1.2.1, Message Trace 1.8.0 -> 1.8.1, Blocked Senders 1.5.0 -> 1.5.1

### Found while doing it, not fixed (one finding per commit) - candidates for the supervisor

1. **Message Trace's live detail download does not stop for the operator's cancel.** `MessageTraceService.GetMessageDetailAsync`
   never throws: `RunDetailBackendAsync` turns every exception, a cancellation included, into a per-message error, and the
   on-premises read's `Task.Run(..., ct)` cancellation becomes "Delivery detail failed: A task was canceled."; the cloud read
   takes no token (kd-58). So once the operator cancels, the loop runs on, the CSV is pushed to the browser and the row is
   filed as a successful download - the page's own comment says the cancel stops the fetch. The cancel catch kd-57 now
   files Cancelled is reached only by a cancellation from the browser call or the frame. Read, not run. Fix shape: honour
   `activity.CancellationToken` in the loop (and let the service rethrow a cancellation of its own token); a behaviour
   change to the download, so its own row. LOW by my reading: read-only, over rows the operator selected.
2. That catch has no filter on the activity's token, so a cancellation the operator did not cause (a browser-call timeout's
   `TaskCanceledException`) is filed as theirs - as before, then under Failed. Narrowing it needs the token outside the
   `using`. LOW; one row with 1.
3. **The Admin Event Log's Result filter cannot select Cancelled.** It offers All, Success, Failed, Ineligible and
   Undetermined (`AdminEventLog.razor` 96-103); a Cancelled row is found only under All, with the warning badge. The kd-65
   class, for the Result filter; aud-a28z part 5 added the Undetermined option together with that result. Not added here:
   the brief said not to change what the operator can do. One `<option>`, a tripwire like `TheResultFilter_OffersUndetermined`
   and an Admin Event Log version bump.
4. `BulkJobService` words a cancelled job "Cancelled by operator." even when the cancel was the host shutting down (its
   linked shutdown token). The job store's words, not an audit row; noted only.
