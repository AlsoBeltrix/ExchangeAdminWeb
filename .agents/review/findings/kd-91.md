**Severity**: HIGH - sweep item 7, seen on dev 2026-10-30 08:21-08:22: four overlapping autocomplete borrows; the first took the

## The fix, part 1 - the pool (implementer, 2026-10-31; branch `fix/sweep-b` from master `81ae0762`)

- **`Services/ExoConnectionPool.cs`**:
  - New `TakePooled(generation, source, out retired)`: the one lending rule, moved unchanged out of `BorrowAsync` - a
    runspace is lent when it was connected under the borrow's generation and has not sat idle past the timeout (cleared,
    traced `ExoConnectionBorrowed` with its source); one that fails the test is traced `ExoConnectionStaleConfig` /
    `ExoConnectionExpired` as before and handed back for the caller to destroy. `BorrowAsync`'s look before the gate calls
    it with source `Pool` and destroys what it hands back exactly as before (aud-a6d's untokened `Task.Run`).
  - `CreateConnected`, once `_connectGate` is held and before the first `Get-ConnectionInformation` snapshot: the second
    look, `TakePooled(generation, "PoolAfterConnectGate", ...)`. A connection returned while the borrow queued is returned
    to it, no connect runs, and in the `finally` - after the gate is released - the session opened for the connect (never
    connected, so nothing to disconnect) is disposed and one Information line is logged ("a connection returned while this
    borrow waited to connect was lent instead"). One the second look takes but cannot lend is destroyed in the same
    `finally`, after the release, so its `Disconnect-ExchangeOnline -ConnectionId` holds up no one else's connect; the borrow
    then connects as before.
  - aud-a6b's identification rule is untouched: the look happens inside the gate and before the "before" snapshot, so the
    two snapshots still bracket only this borrow's own `Connect-ExchangeOnline`. A connection lent from the pool keeps the id
    it was attributed when it was made. Slot accounting is unchanged: the returned connection gave its slot back in
    `Return`, and the borrow that takes it holds its own.
  - The gate's cost remark is corrected: an empty pool - after every restart and every idle sweep - is the common path, not
    the rare one.
- No version bump: the pool is shared (base app), and the base bump is already owed for this stream (brief).

## Found while doing it, not fixed

- **A borrow queued at the gate still waits for the connect ahead of it.** It can look in the pool only once it holds the
  gate, so a connection returned while it waits is lent when the connect in front finishes - on the dev pattern that is one
  connect (about 9 s) instead of k. Waking a queued borrow on a `Return` would remove that wait too; it is a larger change to
  the gate (aud-a6b) and was not made.
- **The look before the gate takes one runspace.** If that one is stale or idle past the timeout it is destroyed and the borrow
  goes on to connect even when another pooled runspace could have been lent; the second look now takes one more. The idle
  sweep (every five minutes) keeps expired ones rare. Pre-existing, recorded only.
