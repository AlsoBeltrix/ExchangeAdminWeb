## Approach (to be written by the implementing agent before code)

The implementing agent's (aud-a70 agent, `fix/a70`, 2026-10-23), written before code.

**This revises aud-a70 (15).** There the agent chose to tell administrators alone of a change filed Undetermined, because the
grant and removal notices say access changed, which nobody can say then. Codex is right that this leaves a change that did
apply silent to the people whose access it altered, which the Constitution does not allow ("Any change to a user's
permissions or access must additionally notify the affected user", `docs/ProjectConstitution.md` 80). What was wrong for them
was the grant notice, not any notice. `aud-a70.md` (15) points here.

Confirmed at `d609001e`: `Services/PermissionChangeNotifier.cs` 30-56 (`NotifyAdminIfUnconfirmedAsync`, administrators
only), called from the four catch sites (`MailboxPermissions.razor` 529 and 747, `CalendarPermissions.razor` 506 and 690).
The success paths (Mailbox 515-521 and 733-738, Calendar 491-498 and 676-681) and the bulk loops
(`MailboxPermissionService.cs` 392, `CalendarPermissionService.cs` 292) tell the user and the owner only of what applied, so
a right whose outcome is unknown (`PermissionResult.UnconfirmedRights`, aud-a70 (14)) tells them nothing.

- The choice: a distinct notice, not verify-then-notify. A verify step would read the permission back over the session that
  had just failed - the same uncertainty, one more remote call on the failure path, and a new way for the action to fail.
- `EmailService` gains `SendUnconfirmedUserNotificationAsync` and `SendUnconfirmedOwnerNotificationAsync` (virtual, and gated
  by `Email:NotifyUsersOnPermissionGrant` as the grant notices are), with one body builder, `BuildUnconfirmedChangeBody`
  (`internal static`, read by tests without SMTP - the `BuildCloudPasswordResetBody` shape).
- **Wording choice.** Subject "Access Change Could Not Be Confirmed". To the user: "A change to your access to the following
  mailbox|calendar was attempted, and whether it took effect could not be confirmed. Your access may or may not have
  changed." To the owner: "A change to the access the following user has to your mailbox|calendar was attempted, and whether
  it took effect could not be confirmed. Their access may or may not have changed." Then the mailbox (or the user), the
  permission, "Change attempted: grant|removal", "Attempted by", the date, and "What to do: To find out whether the access
  changed, or if you did not expect this change, please contact the IT Service Desk." Neutral by design: never "granted" or
  "removed", no reassurance either way, and the one contact point the grant notices already name. A grey header and no emoji,
  so it looks like neither the grant notice nor the removal one.
- `PermissionChangeNotifier.NotifyAdminIfUnconfirmedAsync` becomes `NotifyIfUnconfirmedAsync`: the administrators' notice as
  before, then `NotifyAffectedOfUnconfirmedAsync` (new) - the user and the owner, each send guarded on its own (a throw is
  logged, the other is still sent, the result stands), nothing sent for an empty list. The four catch sites call it in place.
- The success paths and both bulk loops add one call each after the grant notices, on what is unconfirmed:
  `UnconfirmedRights` on Mailbox Permissions (a partial gets the grant notice for what applied and this one for the rest),
  `Unconfirmed` on Calendar Permissions (its one folder permission). The pages stay 808 and 762 lines (each call joins its
  closing brace's line); ProgressRegistry names the new call in both `SubmitSingle` entries.
- Tests: the notifier (every unconfirmed right named, none sent for none, each throw logged and the other still sent), the
  body's wording, both pages' cloud and on-premises unconfirmed writes telling the user and the owner and never sending the
  grant notice, an Exchange refusal telling nobody, the catch-filed case, and source pins on both bulk loops. The (15) tests
  that pinned "no one else" are revised.
- Mutations: M44 the user notice dropped (the brief's); E44a the owner notice dropped; E44b the catch path's notices dropped;
  E44c and E44d each page's success-path call removed; E44e the Mailbox bulk loop's call removed.
- App-wide: `EmailService` and `PermissionChangeNotifier` are shared services, so this is an app-wide change; its behaviour
  is the two permission modules', which the module bumps carry - the csproj base version is not bumped (the brief).
- README (Email Notifications, User Notifications) gains one line for the new notice.
