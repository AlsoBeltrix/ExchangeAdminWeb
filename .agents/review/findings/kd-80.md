# kd-80: a Conference Rooms CSV apply whose job-list reload fails after the job is queued is shown and audited as failed

**Severity**: MEDIUM - Known Failure Class 1 with a duplicate-run consequence. `Components/Pages/ConferenceRooms.razor`
`ApplyFinderCsv` (about 1353) and `ApplyTypeCsv` (about 1673) reload the job list INSIDE their try, after the job has been
queued. If that reload throws, the catch replaces the success banner with the error and writes a Failed "(bulk)" audit row
while the job runs; the submit writes no page row of its own, so the operator, told it failed, may submit the same file
again and queue it twice. Also prose: the page comment at about 649 and ClickGateRegistry remarks at about 3453 and 3486
call the jobs panel's handlers synchronous; all three are async.
**Status**: Fixed on branch, verified; closes on guard proof
**Branch**: `fix/kd80` (worktree `D:\source\ExchangeAdminWeb-wt\kd80`, from master `e5942f76`)
**Commit**: the commit that adds this line (SHA in the implementer's hand-back)
**Reviewer**: implementer (candidate)

## Evidence

At `f8d501d2`: the kd-79 record's "Found, not fixed".

## Approach

- Move the reload out of the try that covers the queue, or give it its own try: once the job is queued, the success
  banner and the queued audit row stand; a reload failure is logged and shown as "the job was queued; the list could not
  be refreshed" without a Failed row. Test per handler with a store that queues then throws on read; mutation: the reload
  moved back inside. Fix the three prose sites. Conference Rooms patch bump; ClickGate re-anchor if lines move.

## Found while doing it, not fixed (one finding per commit)

- **A runner whose first read fails leaves the job just queued Queued until the next submit anywhere.**
  `Services/Jobs/BulkJobService.cs` `DrainQueueAsync` catches a throw from its `GetActive` read at the top of the loop, logs
  "Bulk job pump loop faulted" and stops (`_pumpRunning = false`); only `Enqueue` starts the pump again (`EnsurePump`, its one
  caller), and start-up marks every non-terminal job Interrupted. In this finding's own case - the store failing a read just
  after the write - the runner's first read meets the same failure, so the job the banner now truthfully calls queued may
  not start until another job is submitted, in any module, or be Interrupted at the next restart. The kd-80 test shows the
  mechanism (the runner's read fails and the job stays Queued). Pre-existing, shared service, LOW; a candidate.
- **A reload that fails after a removal that happened reaches the circuit.** `RemoveJob` ends with `await RefreshJobs()`
  (962) outside any try - kd-79 left it there on purpose so a reload failure is not filed as a failed removal - so after a
  removal audited Success, a store failing the reload throws to the circuit instead of the list refreshing. No row is
  misfiled. Pre-existing, LOW; a candidate.
- Prose only, an assumption from reading, not run: ClickGateRegistry's remark that "a future edit reverting RemoveJob to
  take an id and re-look-up the row would pass this whole suite" (3494-3495) and ExemptControl 575's "no assertion in this
  suite holds it either" (3799) look stale. `ConferenceRoomPageTests` (kd-5, kd-27, kd-78, kd-79) hand `RemoveJob` the
  rendered `BulkJob` and check the row's old values, and 575's own snippet pins `() => RemoveJob(job)`; a revert to an id
  would fail both.
- Test timing, not this change: `CircuitRevalidationTests.AnOpenCheckThatTimesOut_RevokesAndDropsTheHeldActivity` (1016)
  failed once in a full run, `Assert.Single()` on the audit finding it empty (1036), and passed alone three times and in
  the re-run. An assumption from reading, not proven: `MembershipRevalidatingAuthenticationStateProvider.Revoke` publishes
  the revocation (`TryRevoke`) before it writes its audit line, and two paths revoke here - the open check at its 1 s lookup
  timeout, and `CircuitRevocationGate.AwaitVerdictAsync`'s backstop at its 2 s hold (`HoldTimeout`, twice the lookup
  timeout). On a starved pool, if the check's `TryRevoke` wins and the backstop then finds the circuit revoked, the
  backstop writes nothing and drops the held activity the test awaits while the check's line is still to be written - the
  one interleaving an empty read fits. Pre-existing; a candidate (the test's read, or the order of publish and audit).

## Known gaps

- After a reload failure the list is not refreshed: the job shows on the next read that works - a job event, a later
  submit or removal, the page's next load - and, while the store stays unreadable, not at all (see the first candidate).
- The banner does not carry the exception's text; the log does. The log line is not asserted (the tests' logger is a null
  logger).
- The note follows the success message, which still says "watch progress below" - true once the list next reads.
- Like every banner on this page, the note stays until dismissed or replaced; a dismiss made while the reload is in flight
  is followed by the note's banner if the reload then fails, since the note is news the dismissed banner did not carry.
- Driven by reflection with substitutes for the authentication state and the policy decision; no renderer.
