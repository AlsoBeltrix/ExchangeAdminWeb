# kd-86: Admin Bulk Jobs reloads its job list after a cancel or removal outside any try

**Severity**: LOW, pre-existing, the kd-82 (2) class. `Components/Pages/AdminBulkJobs.razor` about 357 and 411 reload the
job list after a cancel or a removal that happened (`RefreshJobs` 222-231 -> `LoadJobsFromStore` 201-208) outside any try;
a store failure on that reload reaches the circuit though the audit row is already filed correctly. Conference Rooms' and
Comms-10k's reloads after a cancel are already guarded (kd-79, kd-82), so this closes the class across the three job pages.
**Status**: Fixed on branch, verified; closes on guard proof

## Known gaps

- After a reload failure the job list is not refreshed: a removed job stays listed, and a cancelled one shows as it was,
  until the next read that works - a job event, the Refresh button, another cancel or removal, the page's next load.
- The banner does not carry the exception's text; the log does. Like every banner on this page, it stays until replaced.
- The note is built on the handler's own words (`message`), not on the field read back after the reload's await; no test
  drives another click landing while the reload waits, so that choice is held by the code and its comment, not by a test.
- The page's other reads of the store - the Refresh button (45), the first load (188) and a job's details (`ToggleDetails`,
  291) - still have nothing around them, so a store failure there reaches the circuit, as before. No action and no audit row
  stand behind them, and they are outside this finding, which names the two reloads after a cancel or a removal.
- Driven by reflection with substitutes for the authentication state and the policy decision; no renderer. The stand-ins
  fail the reads with a renamed table, a test device; a locked or unreadable database was not used. The log line is
  asserted through the test's logger; the production sink is not exercised.
