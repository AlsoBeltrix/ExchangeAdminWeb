## Approach

Re-read at `77233d89`:

- `ExchangeAdminWeb.Tests/ClickGateSource.cs:397-398` -
  `for (var at = blanked.IndexOf("<!--", StringComparison.Ordinal); at >= 0; at = blanked.IndexOf("<!--", at + 4, StringComparison.Ordinal))`.
  Both exits of the body leave `at` at the index of the `-->` that closed the comment just blanked (`:411`
  `at = inTemplate;` in the kd-42 template path, `:422` `at = close;` in the markup path). The next iteration then
  searches from `at + 4`, one character past the `-->`, so a comment opening at `at + 3` - written straight after the
  previous one - is never seen and stays readable.

What changes: the search resumes at `at + 1`. A `-->` holds no `<`, so from `at + 1` the next `<!--` is still found at
the comment's very end and nothing can match inside the three characters skipped over; the `continue` paths, which leave
`at` on a `<!--` rather than on a `-->`, also advance by one and still terminate. One line, plus why.

Declared files: `ExchangeAdminWeb.Tests/ClickGateSource.cs`, `ExchangeAdminWeb.Tests/ClickGateTests.cs` (the guard), this
record. No module version: test infrastructure only.

Mutation: `at + 4` restored.
