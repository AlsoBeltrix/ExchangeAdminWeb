**Verified, and wider than recorded.** Three sites parse the type with `Enum.TryParse<RoomType>`:
`Services/Jobs/ConferenceRoomBulkProcessor.cs` 209 (`ApplyTypeRowAsync`, the bulk row's write, ignoring case),
`Components/Pages/ConferenceRooms.razor` 1502 (`HandleTypeCsvUpload`, the CSV preview, ignoring case) and 1398
(`SetSingleRoomType`, the single room, case-sensitive). Measured in-process (pwsh 7.6.6, a framework enum, nothing else):
`Enum.TryParse` accepts "4" (a defined value), "9" (undefined), " 2 " (padded) and "Monday,Tuesday" - a comma list it ORs
into one value, here a defined one. So "reject all-digit input, then `Enum.IsDefined`" would still take a CSV type of
"Video,Restricted" as 3, Exception. The approach's other option, a name switch, closes all of them.
