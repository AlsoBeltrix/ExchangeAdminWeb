# kd-84: the job runner's read-back after a job's final write is outside any try

**Severity**: LOW, pre-existing - `Services/Jobs/BulkJobService.FinishAndNotify` reads the job back (`_repository.Get`, about
line 736) after the final write lands, outside any try. If the store fails that read on the backstop's path, the runner stops
until the next submit (the kd-82/83 class); on the normal path the backstop catches it, but the module's completion hook is
skipped and the log calls it an unexpected fault. Noted alongside: the job's DI scope is created and disposed outside the try
(no scoped service found that this would affect; read, not run).
**Status**: Fixed on branch, verified; closes on guard proof
**Branch**: `fix/kd84` (worktree `D:\source\ExchangeAdminWeb-wt\kd84`, from master `e78d9b9f`)
**Commit**: the commit that adds this line (its SHA in the implementer's hand-back)
**Reviewer**: implementer (candidate)

## Evidence

At `fb847bdc`: the kd-83 record's "Found and not fixed".

## Approach

- Put the read-back and the completion hook inside one notification try: a failed read is logged as a notification failure,
  the job's status stands, the runner continues. Test: a store that fails the read-back once - the runner continues and the
  next job runs, one log line; mutation: the try removed. Shared service; the base bump is already owed for this release.

## The fix (implementer, 2026-10-28; lines at this commit)

Read first, confirmed at `e78d9b9f`: `FinishAndNotify` (695-739 there) makes the final write in kd-83's retry loop, raises
`JobChanged` (732) and, when its compare-and-swap fired, reads the job back - `_repository.Get` (736), a `SELECT` on a
connection of its own (`BulkJobRepository.cs` 400-408) - for `NotifyCompletedInScope` (738), whose try (765-780) covers the
hook's build and run but not the read. A read the store failed threw out of the tail:
- from the job's own try - a processor that cannot be built, no processor, a payload that cannot be parsed, the job's end
  (555, 565, 579, 596) - into the backstop (598-607): "Bulk job {Job} faulted unexpectedly", for a job that had finished,
  then the tail again, Interrupted with "Job faulted: <the store's message>" from Queued or Running - a write that matches
  nothing, the job being finished. The status stood; the hook was not run, and the log named a fault;
- from the backstop's own call (605) out of `RunJobAsync` to the loop's outer catch (510-518): "Bulk job pump loop faulted",
  the flag cleared, the loop ended - the runner stopped until the next submit, and the jobs behind it waited Queued, or for a
  restart to mark them Interrupted.

Reachable as described: M0 below is the file as at `e78d9b9f`, and both tests fail on it - the normal path on its line
("Bulk job j1 faulted unexpectedly"), the backstop's path with the job behind it still Queued.

- **The read in one try with the hook** (`Services/Jobs/BulkJobService.cs` 743-752; the comment 741-742, the remarks
  694-698). The read and the call to the hook are in a try of their own, after the write and `JobChanged` as before. A throw:
  - is logged once, an error with the read's own exception, in the words every failed completion notification is logged in:
    "Completion notification for bulk job {Job} failed (non-fatal)" (751) - as kd-83 (1) logs a hook whose processor cannot
    be built. The Constitution: "Notification failure must not change or mask the backend operation result. Catch
    notification exceptions and log them separately." (`docs/ProjectConstitution.md` 82);
  - leaves the status the write landed as it is: nothing is written after it, and no hook runs, the hook being given the
    job as read;
  - goes no further: the tail returns, so the backstop does not take it, `RunJobAsync` returns, and the loop reads the next
    job.
- **One try in the tail, not in `NotifyCompletedInScope`.** That method keeps its own try for the hook's build and run
  (kd-83 (1)); it also serves the start-up sweep and a queued cancel, which hand it a job they have read
  (`NotifyCompleted`, 764-775). The new try covers the read and the call, so a failure of either is the one line.
- **Unchanged**: a read that works - the same job to the same hook, the same `JobChanged`, no line; a compare-and-swap that
  did not fire (no read, as before); the final write and its retry (kd-83); `NotifyCompletedInScope`, `NotifyCompleted`,
  `TryCancelJob`, `InitializeAsync`, `DrainQueueAsync`.
- **Version**: none here. `BulkJobService` is a shared service, so a base app version bump is owed (2.36.0 at this commit);
  as briefed, it is the supervisor's and is not made here.

**Operator-visible change.** None for a job whose read-back works: every job while the jobs database takes its reads, and a
job that finishes normally is shown, mailed and logged as before. When the database fails the read right after it took a
job's final write, the job is listed with the status it was given and no module completion mail goes for it, both as
before; the log calls it a failed completion notification where it said the job had faulted. And for a job that had
faulted, the runner goes on to the next job where it stopped and left every job behind it Queued until the next submit.

## Found while doing it, not fixed (one finding per commit)

- **A queued job's cancel reads the job back outside any try as well.** `TryCancelJob` (194-200) finishes a queued job as
  Cancelled, then reads it back for its completion hook (`_repository.Get`, 197) outside any try. A store that fails that
  read after it took the cancel throws out of `CancelJob` / `CancelOwnedJob` to the page, though the job was cancelled:
  Admin Bulk Jobs files the cancel as failed with the store's message and throws on to the circuit
  (`AdminBulkJobs.razor` 336-352); Comms-10k says "The job action failed. Refresh to check its current state." and files
  the control as refused or failed (`Comms10k.razor` 1166-1177); and the module's mail for the cancel is not sent. This
  finding's class on the cancel path - a notification failure masking the result. Pre-existing, shared service, LOW (a store
  failing a read right after it took a write); a candidate, from reading, not run.
- **The start-up sweep's notifications stop at the first read that fails.** `InitializeAsync` (143-151) reads each job it
  marked Interrupted back for its hook in a fire-and-forget task, outside any try: a read the store fails ends the loop, so
  the jobs after it get no completion mail, and the task's exception is unobserved - nothing is logged. Pre-existing, shared
  service, LOW; a candidate, from reading, not run.

## Known gaps

- After a failed read the module's completion mail for that job - and Message Analysis' audit row, which its hook files - is
  not sent, as before: the hook is given the job as read, and the read is not made again (the brief: a failed read is a
  notification failure). The log carries it.
- The stand-in fails the read with a renamed table and recovers at the runner's next line, a test device; a locked or
  unreadable database was not used. In production a store that fails that read may fail the next queue read as well, which
  kd-82's retry takes (its warning, or its stop).
- The lines are asserted through the test's logger; the production sink is not exercised.
- Noted at admission and unchanged here: `RunJobAsync`'s scope is still created and disposed outside its try (541); kd-83's
  known gaps read the disposable services and found none that a job's scope disposes.
