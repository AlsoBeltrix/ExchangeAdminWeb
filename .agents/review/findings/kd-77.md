## As implemented (implementer, 2026-10-27; lines at this commit)

Read first, confirmed at `ec5a19f7`: kd-62's `DeleteJob(moduleId, jobId, out outcome)` read the job (`_repository.Get`), checked
its module and then called `_repository.Delete`, whose `DELETE ... WHERE id = $id AND status IN (terminal)` runs in a
transaction of its own; a false from it was taken for NotFinished. The page already words each outcome
(`Components/Pages/AdminBulkJobs.razor` 382-387: NotFound "it no longer exists.", NotThisModule "it belongs to another
module.", otherwise "it has not finished.") and audits the outcome it is given, so only the outcome was wrong.

- **One write transaction** (`Services/Jobs/BulkJobRepository.cs` `Remove`, 337-394). `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`
  (`BeginTransaction(deferred: false)`, the repo's spelling of it, as `SqliteConfigStore` and the migrator use) takes the
  write lock before the read. Inside it: the job read with the repository's own columns and `ReadJob` (no row: NotFound);
  its module compared ordinal and case-insensitive, as the service compared it (another: NotThisModule); then the same
  terminal-only `DELETE` as `Delete` - one row: commit, Removed; none: NotFinished, since under the lock the job exists and
  is this module's, so only its status can have kept it. Nothing is committed unless the job was deleted. A removal that
  loses the race waits for the lock and then reads no job: NotFound.
- **The service** (`Services/Jobs/BulkJobService.cs` 294-301) returns the repository's outcome and raises the change event
  only for a removal, as before. Its kd-62 remarks gain why (289-292). The two-argument overload, Conference Rooms' own
  Remove, keeps its bool (true exactly when Removed). `Delete` stays for `DeleteOwnedJob`.
- **The page is unchanged**: it already words NotFound as such and files the real outcome; with the outcome now right, the
  second administrator's banner reads "Could not remove <module> job <type> - it no longer exists." and its row "The job
  was not removed: it no longer exists.", with kd-54's `refusedAt` "JobState" and `mutationAttempted` false. What the page
  lets the operator do is unchanged.
- Version: Admin Bulk Jobs 1.2.0 -> 1.2.1 (`Modules/ModuleCatalog.cs` 1667-1669). **Base bump owed, not made here**: the bulk
  job store and service are shared infrastructure (Constitution, Deployment And Versioning), the supervisor's call, as for
  kd-57 and kd-62.
