# kd-79: a jobs-store throw during a Conference Rooms job removal leaves no audit row and no banner

**Severity**: LOW, pre-existing - `Components/Pages/ConferenceRooms.razor` `RemoveJob` calls `DeleteJob` outside any try, so a
store throw leaves the handler with no audit row and no banner and the exception reaches the circuit. The hole kd-62 closed
on Admin Bulk Jobs; this page's `CancelJob` already audits its store failure (kd-5).
**Status**: Fixed on branch, verified; closes on guard proof

**Commit**: the commit that adds this line (SHA in the implementer's hand-back)
**Reviewer**: implementer (candidate)

- Wrap `DeleteJob` as `CancelJob` wraps its store call: a throw is audited Failed with the exception's class and message

## As implemented (implementer, 2026-10-27; lines at this commit)

Read first, confirmed at `d8af27a8`: `RemoveJob` (923-945) called the three-argument `BulkJobs.DeleteJob` at 931 outside any
try. `DeleteJob` throws only from the store's one write transaction (`BulkJobRepository.Remove`: open, BEGIN IMMEDIATE, the
read, the module check, the terminal-only delete, the commit), so before or at its commit; the change event after a
committed delete swallows its subscribers' throws (`BulkJobService.RaiseChanged`). A throw therefore left the handler before
`AuditJobControl` and the banner and went to the circuit: no row, no banner. The handler's tail would not have survived it
either: `RefreshJobs` reads the same store (`LoadJobsFromStore`), so on an unreadable jobs database it throws in turn.

- **The wrap** (936-948): the store call alone in a try, as `CancelJob` (837) wraps its own (kd-5) - only the call, so the
  kd-78 tail's own failures (a reload that throws after a removal that happened) are not filed as a failed removal. `removed`
  and `outcome` are declared above it (936-937). The catch:
  - logs the exception with its stack (944), which the circuit's unhandled-exception log carried before;
  - writes the row through `AuditJobControl` (945): Failed, "The removal could not be completed: <class>: <message>"
    (`ex.GetType().Name`, `ex.Message`), and no `refusedAt` or `mutationAttempted` - a throw is not a refusal, the store
    may have been reached: kd-5's and kd-32's rule on this page, kd-62's on Admin Bulk Jobs;
  - sets the banner (946): "The removal of the <kind> job could not be completed.", naming the job by `JobKindLabel` as
    kd-78's banner does. It is a direct `new RoomOperationResult { ... }`, so ClickGateRegistry's remark that the scanner
    sees no bare-token raise of `result` stays true;
  - returns (947): the exception goes no further, and the list is not reloaded from the store that has just failed - the
    throw skipped that reload before as well. This is where it departs from `CancelJob` and kd-62, both of which throw on
    after the row: the brief's "the exception does not escape".
- **Unchanged**: the access re-check first (kd-27); a removal that happens - its row, the banner left as it was, the reload -
  and kd-78's not-removed outcomes - the reason, the row with its refusal fields, the banner - are the lines below the try
  (949-961), as kd-78 left them.
- **Registries**: ProgressRegistry's `RemoveJob` exemption (a single local job-store delete after the access re-check; the
  refresh it triggers registered on `RefreshJobs`) and ClickGateRegistry's 575 condition (the rendered row, no lookup) stay
  true as written.
- **Lines**: 1791 -> 1808, one hunk at 931 (+17), below every line-keyed entry (the last at 575): ClickGateRegistry's
  `ExpectedLineCount` 1791 -> 1808, no entry re-anchored.
- **Version**: Conference Rooms 2.19.6 -> 2.19.7 (`Modules/ModuleCatalog.cs` 1078-1081), its note naming kd-79. The page is
  the module's own; no base bump.

**Operator-visible change.** Removing a Conference Rooms bulk job while the jobs store fails - a locked or unreadable jobs
database - shows "The removal of the Room Type (bulk) job could not be completed." (or "Room Finder (bulk)") where the
circuit's error was, and the audit has a row for it: Failed, "The removal could not be completed: <class>: <message>", no
refusal fields. The job list stays as it was shown. A removal that happens, and one the store refuses, are unchanged.

## Found while doing it, not fixed (one finding per commit)

- **A CSV apply whose job list reload throws is filed and shown as a failed submit.** `ApplyFinderCsv` and `ApplyTypeCsv`
  await `RefreshJobs` inside their try, after `EnqueueConferenceRoomJob` has queued the job and the success banner is set
  (1353 and 1673 at this commit). A throw from that reload - the jobs store failing a read just after the write - reaches
  the catch, which replaces the banner with the exception's message (Success false) and writes a Failed
  `ConferenceRooms_SetMetadata` / `ConferenceRooms_SetType` "(bulk)" row, while the queued job runs. The submit itself
  writes no page row, so that Failed row is the page's only record of it, and the operator, told it failed, may submit it
  again - the duplicate ClickGateRegistry's `RefreshJobs` guard-site entry warns of. Known Failure Class 1. Pre-existing,
  LOW (a read failing right after a successful write); a candidate.
- Prose only, not a defect: the page's comment above `IsBusy` (649) and ClickGateRegistry's remarks on this page (3453,
  3486) still call the jobs panel's handlers synchronous with no await; all three are `async Task` now - the ProgressCoverage
  work's yield and reload, kd-27's access re-check. Their conclusion - no busy flag there for a predicate to read - holds.

## Known gaps

- After a store failure the list is not reloaded, so the job stays listed as it was shown; the next reload (a later
  removal or submit, a job event, the page's next load) shows what the store holds.
- Like every banner on this page, the failure banner stays until dismissed or replaced.
- The banner does not carry the exception's text; the audit row and the log do.
- Driven by reflection with substitutes for the authentication state and the policy decision; no renderer. The log line
  is not asserted (the tests' logger is a null logger).
